Somplo Rich Media Ads

Client: Somplo
Year: 2025
Creative Execution: Oleksandr Kukharyk
EXPLORE FULL CASE
01 / OVERVIEW

THE CHALLENGE

Design and develop interactive Rich Media display ads for global brand campaigns — merging high-end visual identity with real-time interactive mechanics that push standard banners into engagement-driven digital experiences.

About the Project

While working as a Motion Designer at Somplo, I also was responsible for the end-to-end development of interactive Rich Media display ads for high-profile brand campaigns. These creatives were built on Somplo's proprietary platform and distributed through programmatic networks to audiences worldwide.

The campaigns spanned a wide range of global brands — including Giorgio Armani, L'Oréal Group (Elseve, Garnier, Maybelline), Mazda, Ralph Lauren, Lottusse, Toyota, Nestlé and others. Each creative was precision-crafted to mirror the brand's visual identity and leverage Somplo's proprietary interactive formats — from 3D cube rotations and parallax effects to scratch overlays and multi-product carousels.

02 / WORKFLOW

MY ROLE & APPROACH

As part of the Somplo in-house team, I worked across the full creative pipeline — from visual concept and motion design to final interactive build — producing brand campaign ads tailored to each client's identity within Somplo's proprietary Rich Media platform.

Design

Visual concept, brand adaptation, composition, colour grading, and motion storyboards for each format.

Animation

Frame-by-frame motion assets, image assets, timeline coordination across multiple banner sizes.

Interactive Build

Rich Media ad production within the Somplo platform — RichBox, VideoBox, Multisize, and cross-format delivery.
