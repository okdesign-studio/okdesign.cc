01 / Overview

THE CHALLENGE

Van Calster Vloeren & Badkamerrenovatie, a prominent Flemish retail and renovation brand, approached me for their "Dubbel Voordeel" (Double Benefit) promotion — offering free demolition plus a 40% discount on sanitaryware. The brief was wide open: create a key visual from scratch, with emotion and a touch of exaggeration.

About the Project

My usual workflow is to develop a single anchor key visual — then scale it across the entire campaign: print, GDN banners, SMM, website content, interactive before/after, and animated 10-second cuts adapted for social and YouTube. The key visual becomes the source of truth for every format.

This wasn't the first campaign I'd done for Van Calster — so when the client asked me to come up with the concept entirely from scratch and specifically wanted something with an emotion or a touch of exaggeration, I had enough context to go somewhere unexpected.

During the ideation process I arrived at a cinematic horror concept — and decided straight away to think in terms of animation, not a static image, since the idea was inherently about motion and atmosphere. So instead of a key visual, I developed a full advertising spot with voiceover.

After the presentation, the client was so impressed they decided to air it on RTV and RTV+ in Flanders during prime time. I received the broadcast technical specs from the TV channels and produced the final version for television.

03 / Script & Storyboard

CONCEPT &
STORYTELLING

Every scene was meticulously scripted in a storyboard in Figma — from narrative arc to shot transitions — before a single frame of footage was generated or composited.

Storyboard — Figma export Full scene-by-scene breakdown with timing notes

"The same small exaggeration and emotion the client asked for — I decided to express through the atmosphere of cinematic horror."

01
Hook
A gloomy, dated bathroom. Flickering light. An uneasy silence. Then the question drops over the image: "Is your bathroom renovation turning into a real horror movie?" — pulling the viewer in from the very first second.
02
Build-up
The camera pans slowly left to a cracked tile wall. A flashlight beam cuts through the darkness, landing on a message scrawled on the surface: "Leave it to the professionals." Tension mounts.
03
Brand Reveal — Breaking the Fourth Wall
Suddenly, the camera drops to the floor. Suspense hits its peak. The light flickers — then cuts out entirely. A beat of pure darkness. Then: a sharp, bright cut. A renovation worker activates a camera on-site, which simultaneously breaks the fourth wall and mirrors the visual language of real job footage. The voiceover steps in, confident: "Van Calster takes care of everything."
04
The Offer
We reveal the double benefit. A shot of the old bathtub being carried out perfectly underscores the "Gratis uitbraak" (free demolition) line. Then a reveal of a brand-new bathroom interior with sanitaryware, and the voiceover delivers the second punch: 40% op sanitair.
05
CTA & Packshot
The voiceover rounds out the headline: "Badkamerrenovatie met dubbele voordelen." The packshot appears — clean, confident, branded — closing the spot with clarity after all the atmosphere.
04 / Video Production

AI-DRIVEN
PRODUCTION

The visual foundation of the spot was built through AI image generation — using detailed prompts, real references, and state-of-the-art models to produce key frames and scene assets, with strict attention to the specifics of bathroom renovation.

All sanitaryware and key installation elements are real products sourced from the client's price catalog — generated from those references to maintain authenticity. Once the key frames were locked, I used top-tier AI video generation models to animate each scene from those stills, applying detailed motion prompts to achieve the cinematic horror feel.

Some scenes were built entirely in After Effects without AI generation — most notably the flashlight beam scene with its shadow animation and atmospheric light flicker. This allowed for frame-accurate control over the pacing and tension of those critical moments.

05 / Sound Design

AUDIO BUILDS
THE HORROR

Sound design carries the lion's share of the atmosphere in this spot. Every layer was carefully selected and mixed — from the horror ambient bed to each individual SFX hit.

The audio stack is built on a fitting horror ambient foundation with dynamic SFX precisely timed to each cut and visual beat. The voiceover was generated via ElevenLabs using a voice profile as close to Flemish Dutch as possible, then organically mixed into the final audio track.
