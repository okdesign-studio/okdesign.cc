Mars 2050 Board Game Promo & SMM Identity

Client: Ranok
Year: 2021
Creative Execution: Oleksandr Kukharyk
Explore full case
01 / Overview

The challenge

Introduce the Mars 2050 board game in a niche yet competitive market and build a recognisable, thematic social media identity across every launch touchpoint.

About the client

Ranok Publishing House is a leader in Ukraine’s educational, children’s, and methodological literature market. Its portfolio includes more than 800 unique titles across genres, topics, and formats.

As a full-cycle publisher with its own printing house and a chain of bookstores, Ranok reaches readers in dozens of languages worldwide.

My role in the project

Team Lead Designer

  • Facilitated coordination between departments to ensure successful outcomes.
  • Defined team tasks and monitored their timely execution.
  • Created the key design solutions.
  • Acted as Creative Producer for the promo video.
02 / Objectives

Objectives

Create a focused launch system that could explain the product, build recognition, and make the game feel distinctive from the first post.

01

Promotion

Introduce the Mars 2050 board game within a niche but competitive market.

02

Create a recognisable visual concept

Develop a thematic and engaging style that works consistently across all platforms.

03 / Strategy

Strategy & implementation

A coordinated launch approach connected audience insights, channel planning, and a single visual language.

Strategy development

Collaborated with company departments to develop and implement a promotion strategy that included:

  • Segmenting the target audience.
  • Developing content plans for Instagram and Facebook.
  • Creating visual content aligned with the Tone of Voice.
04 / Content production

Photoshoots

I helped organise two shoots: product photography focused on the game components, cards, and packaging, plus a lifestyle shoot with players in a board game café setting.

05 / Promo video

Production

Creative production covered the full process — from understanding the game mechanics and building the story to filming, motion graphics, colour, and sound.

  1. Conducted a briefing with the client.
  2. Analysed the game mechanics to identify the key elements for the video script.
  3. Created a storyboard and received client approval.
  4. Prepared a brief for the copywriter to develop the voice-over script.
  5. Held casting for a voice-over artist to match the required tone and style.
  6. Participated in filming, including selecting references and props and shooting the board, box, and game components.
  7. Completed post-production: editing, motion graphics, colour correction, retouching, and sound design.
